BlushMirror: Makeup Light, published by Infinity Group

BlushMirror turns your phone's front camera into a lit, zoomable makeup mirror. If something isn't working, the answers below cover the most common questions.

Contact us

Email blushmirror.info@gmail.com. We usually reply within three business days. Please include your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

The mirror is black

BlushMirror needs permission to use the camera. Open your phone's Settings, find BlushMirror, and turn Camera on. Then close the app fully and open it again.

The picture is too dark

Turn on the built-in light and raise its strength. Choose warm, daylight or cool to suit the room. Raising your screen brightness also makes the light stronger.

My face looks flipped

A mirror shows you reversed. Turn on True view to see yourself the way other people see you.

How do I compare before and after?

Tap Save "before" at the start. When you're finished, open Compare and slide the divider across your face. The "before" picture is kept only while the app is open.

Does BlushMirror save or upload my camera picture?

No. The camera picture stays on your phone. BlushMirror does not record, upload or share anything, and it has no accounts, ads or analytics.

How to ask for a refund
