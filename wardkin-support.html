Getting started

  • Add the person you look after under More, People.
  • Add their medications with the times they are taken. Tick each dose on the Today screen.
  • Add bills, visits and renewals. Wardkin shows what needs attention this week.
  • Allow notifications when asked so reminders can appear.

Common questions

I am not getting reminders

Open the phone's Settings, find Wardkin and allow notifications. Then open Wardkin, More, Settings and tap Allow.

The emergency number is wrong for my country

Go to More, Settings, choose your country and check the number. You can type the correct one.

I forgot my Wardkin passcode

On the lock screen choose "Forgot passcode". This erases the app's records. You can then restore a backup copy under More, Settings.

I have a new phone

On the old phone make a backup copy (More, Settings) and send it to yourself. On the new phone choose Restore and paste it. Letter photos are not included in backups.

Can my family see the same records?

Not automatically in this version. Use "Share a summary" to send the current details to family or a doctor.

Refund policy

Wardkin is a one-time purchase. We do not give refunds for change of mind. We do give refunds when the app has a genuine problem, as set out below.

When a refund will be accepted

  • The app does not work. It will not open or its main features do not work on a supported phone, and we cannot fix it for you.
  • It is significantly different from its description. A feature described on the store page is missing or does not do what the description says.
  • A problem we could not fix in a reasonable time. If you report a smaller fault, we will first try to fix it with advice or an app update. If we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, you can have a refund.
  • You were charged in error. For example, you were charged twice for the same purchase, or the purchase was made without your permission.

When a refund will not be accepted

  • You changed your mind, or no longer want or need the app.
  • You found a similar app at a lower price or for free.
  • You bought the app by choice and simply do not like it, where it works as described.
  • The problem is caused by something outside the app, such as a damaged phone or a phone that does not meet the requirements shown on the store page.

How to ask for a refund

  • iPhone (App Store): Apple handles all App Store refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose "Request a refund". We cannot issue App Store refunds ourselves, but email us and we will help you with the problem.
  • Android (Google Play), within 48 hours of buying: request the refund through Google Play.
  • Android (Google Play), after 48 hours: email us at kinward.infos@gmail.com with your Google Play order number, your phone model and a short description of the problem.

Your rights under consumer law

This policy is in addition to your legal rights and does not replace or limit them. In Australia, our app comes with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. If the app has a major problem, you are entitled to a refund. If the problem is not major, you are entitled to have it fixed within a reasonable time. If you live outside Australia, you may have other rights under your local law.
