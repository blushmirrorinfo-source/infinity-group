Wardkin privacy policy

App: Wardkin: Family Care (com.infinitygroup.kinward)
Last updated 21 November 2026

The app is published by Infinity Architects & Builders Pty Ltd (ABN 89 686 504 790), trading as Infinity Group. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean that company.

The short version

Wardkin does not collect your information. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We have no servers that receive it, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

What you enter

You may enter details about people you look after and about yourself: names, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, health conditions, allergies, medications, appointments, bills, renewals, contacts, care notes and photos of letters. Some of this is sensitive health information. All of it is stored only on your device, inside the app.

What we receive and share

Nothing. We cannot see, read or recover what you enter, so we do not use it, sell it or share it with anyone.

When information leaves your phone

  • Share and backup: only when you choose to share a summary or make a backup copy, and only to the place you choose.
  • Your phone's own backup: if you use iCloud or Google device backup, your phone maker may include the app's data in that backup under its own privacy terms.
  • Emergency button: opens your phone's dialler. The call is made by your phone, not by Wardkin.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders you set. They are created on your phone.
  • Camera and photos: to photograph a letter you want to keep. Photos stay in the app on your phone.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint: to unlock the app if you turn that on. Wardkin never receives your face or fingerprint data; your phone only tells the app whether the check passed.

Keeping it safe

You can lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or a passcode. This locks the screen; it does not encrypt the stored data. Keep your phone itself locked with a passcode.

How long information is kept, and deleting it

Your information stays on your phone until you remove it. Use "Delete everything on this phone" in Settings, or delete the app. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to keep or delete.

Purchases

Wardkin is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your card details.

If you email us

If you write to our support address, we receive your email address and whatever you tell us. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation, and you can ask us to delete it.

Children

Wardkin is intended for adults who look after family members. It is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Wardkin is an organiser. It does not provide medical, legal or financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app stores information online, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the app will ask you first.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: kinward.infos@gmail.com. We usually reply within three business days.
