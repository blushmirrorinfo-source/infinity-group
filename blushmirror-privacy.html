BlushMirror privacy policy

App: BlushMirror: Makeup Light (com.blushmirror.app)
Last updated 21 November 2026

The app is published by Infinity Architects & Builders Pty Ltd (ABN 89 686 504 790), trading as Infinity Group. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean that company.

BlushMirror turns your phone's front camera into a makeup mirror. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Camera

  • The app uses the camera only to show a live mirror image on your screen.
  • The camera image is not recorded, not saved to our servers, not uploaded and not shared with anyone.
  • A "before" snapshot or a frozen still is kept only in the app's memory while the app is open, and is discarded when you close the app.

Information we collect and share

None. BlushMirror has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. It does not collect, store or share personal information.

How long information is kept, and deleting it

We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Closing the app discards any snapshot it was holding.

Purchases

BlushMirror is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your card details.

If you email us

If you write to our support address, we receive your email address and whatever you tell us. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation, and you can ask us to delete it.

Children

BlushMirror is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: blushmirror.info@gmail.com. We usually reply within three business days.
