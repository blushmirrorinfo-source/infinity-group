About Infinity Group

Infinity Group is the name we publish our mobile apps under. It is a trading name of Infinity Architects & Builders Pty Ltd, an Australian company based in Punchbowl, New South Wales.

What we make

We make small apps for iPhone and Android that each do one job. Our current apps are Wardkin: Family Care, an organiser for people looking after an ageing parent, and BlushMirror: Makeup Light, which turns a phone into a lit mirror. Both are described on the home page.

How we build

What you enter in our apps stays on your phone. Our apps have no advertising and no tracking, and they are paid for once, with no subscription. A real person answers every support email.

Our other work

The same company also runs a group of online stores.
