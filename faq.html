Where can I get the apps?

Our apps are published through the Apple App Store and Google Play. If you cannot find an app in your store or country yet, email that app's support address on the Contact page and we will tell you where it stands.

What do the apps cost?

Each app is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and there are no in-app advertisements. The current price is shown on the app's store page.

Do the apps collect my information?

No. What you enter stays on your phone, and we cannot see it. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from the home page.

Do I need an account?

No. Our apps do not ask you to create an account or sign in.

How do I get help?

Email the support address for the app, shown on the Contact page. A real person answers, usually within two business days.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play, and refund requests are handled by those stores. We do not offer refunds for change of mind. This does not limit your rights under consumer law, including the Australian Consumer Law. Each app's support page explains its refund policy in full.

Is Wardkin medical advice?

No. Wardkin is an organiser, not medical advice. Do not rely on reminders alone for critical medication, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.
